# Simonctech

Simón's personal corner of the internet: a Spanish-language site, written mainly for peers and viewers, that shows what he builds, writes, records and is into. Recruiters are a secondary audience.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of work Simón chose to show and wrote up himself, software or hardware. It may link to a repository, but it is not one.
_Avoid_: Repo, repository (as a synonym)

**Post**:
A written article on the blog.
_Avoid_: Article, entry

**Video**:
An upload on Simón's YouTube channel. Videos appear on the site automatically; nobody picks them.
_Avoid_: Clip, episode

**Featured Project**:
A Project Simón marked to be shown first.

**Tag**:
A topic label shared by Posts and Projects, so one topic gathers both.
_Avoid_: Category, label

**Latest feed**:
The mix of the newest Post, Video and Project shown on the home page.
_Avoid_: Timeline, activity

**Uses**:
The part of "Sobre mí" listing Simón's setup, gear and tools, grouped. Shown to visitors as "Con qué trabajo".
_Avoid_: Stack, setup page

**Now**:
The part of "Sobre mí" saying what Simón is focused on at the moment.
